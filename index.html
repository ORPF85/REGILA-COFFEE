Our Story: Sourced by Passion, Roasted by Art

It all began in the winter of 2018 in a small kitchen corner with nothing but an old popcorn popper, a handful of green coffee beans, and an uncompromising dream to hunt down the perfect morning cup. What started as an individual obsession with roasting times and crack cycles quickly turned into an absolute mission to bring souls together over genuinely remarkable coffee environments.

We traveled across steep misty valleys to partner directly with local micro-lot family farms, ensuring our beans are sustainably harvested by hand and ethically traded. Today, our master roasters listen intently to the distinct 'first crack' of the heating profile cycles daily, unlocking hidden flavor signatures of caramelized cane sugar, bright citrus zest, and rich velvety dark cacao chocolate. Every single pour, every meticulous grind, and every warm cup we serve is a chapter of that ongoing story—crafted purposefully for your senses.
